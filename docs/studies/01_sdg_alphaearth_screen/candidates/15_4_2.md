# SDG 15.4.2 — (a) Mountain Green Cover Index and (b) Proportion of Degraded Mountain Land

**Custodian:** FAO.
**Architecture letter:** **A** for both (a) and (b).
**Verdict:** **IN**. (b) was added in the 2025 Comprehensive Review.

## Indicator (post-2025-review text)

> (a) Mountain Green Cover Index and (b) proportion of degraded
> mountain land.

## Measurement target

The mountain reference mask is fixed: UNEP-WCMC's *Mountain Watch*
classification of global mountain area (Kapos et al. 2000), six classes
by elevation, slope, and local relief. Both (a) and (b) are computed
*within* this mask.

**(a) Mountain Green Cover Index (MGCI)**: ratio of "green vegetation"
mountain pixels to total mountain pixels, per country, per year.
Custodian methodology accepts MODIS MCD12Q1 IGBP classes (forest,
shrubland, grassland, cropland) as "green" and the remainder
(barren, snow/ice, water, settlements) as "non-green." Reporting is
annual.

**(b) Proportion of degraded mountain land**: fraction of the mountain
mask classified as degraded over a defined reference period. The
underlying methodology aligns with SDG 15.3.1 (one-out-all-out across
LULC change, productivity decline, soil organic carbon decline) but
restricted to the mountain mask.

**Ancillary non-EO data:** none for either component (the mountain
mask is itself a static spatial product).

## Classification target

**(a) MGCI:** binary per-pixel within mountain mask — *green* vs
*non-green*. The "green" class follows MODIS IGBP land-cover
aggregation: forest (1–5), shrubland (6, 7), savanna (8, 9),
grassland (10), cropland (12, 14). Non-green: barren (16), urban (13),
permanent water (17), permanent snow/ice (15).

**(b) Degraded mountain land:** binary per-pixel within mountain
mask — *degraded* vs *not degraded*. For Study 01 scope, the LULC
change sub-component is the AE-classifiable feeder; the productivity
and SOC sub-components are continuous and out of scope here. Full
indicator value requires composing the three sub-components per
UNCCD's one-out-all-out rule (Architecture F under Study 02).

## Architecture choice

**A is justified for both (a) and (b)** because:

- The custodian methodology accepts MODIS MCD12Q1 (500 m) as the
  *current* operational product. Moving to AE-derived classification
  at 10 m is a substantive resolution improvement that materially
  affects the index value in landscapes where mountain pixels are
  small/fragmented (e.g., Andes, Caucasus, Himalayan foothills).
- The aggregation from native AE classification to "green / non-green"
  is a label remap, not a new modelling problem.
- For (b) specifically, the LULC change sub-component requires
  consistent classification across two (or more) AE annual epochs;
  AE's annual cadence is a direct fit.

**C is also viable for (a)** by directly aggregating ESA WorldCover or
MCD12Q1 per the custodian's class aggregation rule. Use C when the
analyst needs only a quick national-level estimate; use A when
sub-national or change analysis matters.

## Validation strategy

- **Reference for (a):** FAO MGCI national submissions; high-resolution
  photo-interpretation following Olofsson et al. 2014 stratified by
  mountain Kapos class.
- **Reference for (b):** UNCCD national reporting on SDG 15.3.1
  restricted to mountain pixels; Trends.Earth sub-national outputs.
- **Cross-product comparison:** AE-classified green / non-green vs
  MCD12Q1 aggregated / vs ESA WorldCover aggregated.
- **Confusion classes:** seasonal grasslands (spring-green / summer-dry
  alpine pastures), snow-on-vegetation (false non-green in winter
  acquisitions), bare rock with sparse alpine cushion plants (the
  borderline of "moss & lichen" class 100 vs "bare" class 60).
- **Stratification:** by Kapos mountain class (lower bias risk for
  classes 1–3, higher for 4–6 where sub-pixel mixing dominates).

## Caveats

1. **MGCI aggregation rule is methodologically loaded.** The "green"
   class is defined by the custodian as forest + shrubland + grassland
   + cropland. Including cropland is debated: agricultural intensification
   in mountain valleys can increase MGCI without representing
   ecological gain. Document the aggregation choice in any reported
   value.
2. **For (b), the LULC change sub-component alone is not the full
   indicator.** Reporting (b) requires the productivity (continuous EO)
   and SOC (continuous; mostly modelled) sub-components per UNCCD's
   one-out-all-out rule. Study 01 scope provides the LULC change
   feeder; full indicator value requires Study 02's Architecture F
   composer.
3. **Mountain mask granularity.** UNEP-WCMC's Kapos mask is at varying
   native resolutions; rasterising to 10 m introduces small boundary
   artefacts that affect total mountain pixel counts.
4. **Snow and ice ambiguity.** Class 70 (snow/ice) is non-green by
   definition. Trends in mountain snow/ice extent (a separate climate
   signal) systematically bias year-on-year MGCI; report MGCI on a
   snow-cover-corrected mask if multi-year trends are the analytical
   question.
5. **Pre-2017 baseline.** Same caveat as 15.1.1 — the AE era starts
   2017; pre-2017 trajectory must use MCD12Q1 or Landsat-era products.
