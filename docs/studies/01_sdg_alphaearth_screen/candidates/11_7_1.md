# SDG 11.7.1 — Average Share of the Built-up Area of Cities That Is Open Space for Public Use

**Custodian:** UN-Habitat.
**Architecture letter:** **F** (composite); sub-components are A
(built-up) + D (OSM open-space).
**Verdict:** **PARTIAL** — built-up component fits Study 01 narrow
scope; "open space for public use" component is hybrid and best
treated under Study 02.

## Indicator (post-2025-review text)

> Average share of the built-up area of cities that is open space for
> public use for all, by sex, age and persons with disabilities.

## Measurement target

UN-Habitat methodology computes per city:

```
share = total_open_public_space_area / total_built_up_area
```

Aggregated across cities for the country average. Open spaces
include public parks, plazas, playgrounds, and public-access streets.

## Architecture choice

**A** for the built-up extent component (same architecture as 11.3.1).

**D** for the open-public-space component:

- OSM tags `leisure=park`, `leisure=playground`, `landuse=recreation_ground`,
  `amenity=public_park` rasterised to the AOI.
- Plazas and pedestrian-accessible streets from `highway=pedestrian`,
  `highway=living_street`.

**F** overall, composing the two.

The "for all, by sex, age and persons with disabilities" disaggregation
is not EO-mappable; it relies on accessibility audits and survey data.

## Why this is PARTIAL

- The open-public-space classification is not a per-pixel
  AE-classifiable phenomenon — public-vs-private use is not visually
  distinctive in optical/SAR imagery. AE classification cannot
  replace OSM rasterisation for this layer.
- The full indicator therefore needs Architecture F
  (composer) + Architecture D (OSM rasterisation). Study 01's narrow
  AE-classification scope does not cover this.

## Candidate datasets for the hybrid pipeline

| Source | Role |
|---|---|
| AE classification of built-up extent | Built-up denominator (per 11.3.1) |
| OSM (community catalog `projects/sat-io/open-datasets/OSM/...` `[unverified]`) | Public-space numerator |
| GHSL Built-up Surface | Cross-check on built-up denominator |
| Google Open Buildings | Optional building-footprint mask to refine built-up |

## Recommendation

Build the AE-built-up component under 11.3.1's pipeline. Defer the
open-public-space rasterisation and composer to Study 02.
