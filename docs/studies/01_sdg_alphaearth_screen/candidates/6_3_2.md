# SDG 6.3.2 — Proportion of Bodies of Water with Good Ambient Water Quality

**Custodian:** UNEP.
**Architecture letter:** **C** primary; **A** possible if
discrete-class label products mature; **F** if combined with in-situ.
**Verdict:** **PARTIAL** — passes C1–C4 with caveats; C5 weak under
strict reading because no catalog dataset implements UNEP's
discrete water-quality classification at the spatial-temporal
frequency the methodology requires.

## Indicator (post-2025-review text)

> Proportion of bodies of water with good ambient water quality.

## Measurement target

UNEP's methodology defines "good" ambient water quality by parameter
classes (DO, EC, pH, nitrate-N, phosphate-P) measured per water body.
Country reports a percentage of monitored water bodies meeting
"good" criteria.

EO-derived parameters that map to the indicator: chlorophyll-a (Chl-a)
as a eutrophication proxy; turbidity / TSS as a pollution proxy;
surface temperature as a thermal-stress proxy. None of these alone
delivers the indicator; they feed it.

## Why this is PARTIAL, not IN

- **C1 (discrete-class)**: UNEP methodology *defines* discrete classes
  (good / moderate / poor). Pass.
- **C2 (AE-encoded)**: Inland water Chl-a / turbidity signal is partly
  encoded in AE embeddings; AE was not trained explicitly for water
  quality. Weak pass.
- **C3 (annual cadence)**: methodology accepts annual aggregation. Pass.
- **C4 (area / count derived)**: indicator is proportion of *bodies*
  meeting criteria — not area, but countable. Pass with framing
  caveat.
- **C5 (catalog label with AE overlap)**: **FAIL.** No catalog dataset
  classifies inland water bodies into UNEP's "good / moderate / poor"
  classes globally with AE overlap. Continuous chl-a / turbidity
  products exist (Sentinel-3 OLCI, OC-CCI, MODIS) but the
  classification step requires custodian-defined thresholds applied
  to in-situ-calibrated retrievals.

## Recommendation

Defer 6.3.2 implementation until either (a) UNEP releases a
discrete-class EO product within an AE-overlapping window, or (b) the
project commits to threshold-based Architecture C using continuous
chl-a + turbidity products with documented threshold choices.
