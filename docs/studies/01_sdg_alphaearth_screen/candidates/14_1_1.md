# SDG 14.1.1 — (a) Index of Coastal Eutrophication; and (b) Plastic Debris Density

**Custodian:** UNEP.
**Architecture letter:** **F** (composite); (a) component is **C**;
(b) is research-stage with no GEE primitive.
**Verdict:** **PARTIAL**.

## Indicator (post-2025-review text)

> (a) Index of coastal eutrophication; and (b) plastic debris density.

## Component (a) — Coastal Eutrophication Index

**UNEP methodology**: chlorophyll-a anomaly above a baseline,
classified into eutrophication classes (oligotrophic / mesotrophic /
eutrophic / hypertrophic) per coastal pixel and aggregated per AOI.

**Architecture C** (direct catalog aggregation):
- Sentinel-3 OLCI Chl-a or NASA OB.DAAC MODIS-Aqua Chl-a or ESA
  OC-CCI merged Chl-a, thresholded against the UNEP-defined
  eutrophication classes.
- AE-classification adds little; Chl-a retrievals are already the
  custodian-accepted product.

## Component (b) — Plastic Debris Density

Research-stage. Sentinel-2-derived plastic detection is published
(Biermann et al. 2020 *Sci Rep*) but no operational global GEE
catalog product exists. UNEP methodology is currently methodology-
under-development; some submissions use beach-survey data only.

**Architecture: not yet operational on GEE.**

## Why this is PARTIAL not IN

- (a) is C, not A — AE classification adds nothing over thresholded
  Chl-a anomaly catalog products.
- (b) has no GEE primitive at present.

## Recommendation

Implement (a) as Architecture C in Study 02. Track (b) as
research-stage; revisit when a global plastic-debris GEE catalog
product appears.
