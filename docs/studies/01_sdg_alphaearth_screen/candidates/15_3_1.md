# SDG 15.3.1 — Proportion of Land That Is Degraded over Total Land Area

**Custodian:** UNCCD.
**Architecture letter:** **F** in full; **A** for the LULC-change
sub-indicator only.
**Verdict:** **IN** (LULC sub-component); **F** for the full composite
under Study 02.

## Indicator (post-2025-review text)

> Proportion of land that is degraded over total land area.

## Measurement target

UNCCD's *Good Practice Guidance v2* defines the indicator as the
proportion of land area classified as degraded under a **one-out-all-out**
rule across three sub-indicators:

1. **Trends in land cover** — change between land-cover classes is
   classified as degradation, stable, or improvement using a country-
   defined transition matrix.
2. **Trends in land productivity** — typically NDVI or other VI-derived
   trend over a defined reference period; pixels with significant
   negative trend are degraded.
3. **Trends in carbon stocks above and below ground** — soil organic
   carbon (SOC) trend and biomass change.

A pixel is classified as degraded if **any** sub-indicator flags it as
degraded. AOI is the country; reporting is at 4-year intervals.

**Ancillary non-EO data:** SoilGrids predictions for SOC; FAO and IPCC
default values for biomass where direct EO biomass is unavailable.

## Classification target (Study 01 scope = sub-indicator 1 only)

Per-pixel multi-class land cover, classified annually, with
**transitions** between annual classifications scored against a
country-defined degradation transition matrix. Default matrix
(UNCCD-supplied): forest → cropland = degradation; cropland → urban =
degradation; bare → vegetation = improvement; etc.

The full set of land-cover classes follows the UNCCD-recommended
6-class aggregation (tree-covered, grassland, cropland, wetland,
artificial, other land) or the 7-class FAO-LCCS variant.

## Architecture choice

**F overall**, with **A** as the primary architecture for sub-indicator
1.

The full indicator requires composing three sub-indicators of
heterogeneous types — discrete-class (sub-indicator 1) plus continuous
(sub-indicators 2 and 3). A composer that calls the sub-indicator
functions and applies the one-out-all-out rule (Architecture F) is
the operational deliverable. Within that, sub-indicator 1 is naturally
Architecture A: per-pixel AE classification annually, transitions
scored against the country matrix.

For Study 01 scope, the LULC sub-component alone is in scope and is
the focus of this candidate file. Sub-indicators 2 and 3 are tracked
in `../02_sdg_gee_architecture_screen/`.

## Validation strategy

- **Reference:** UNCCD country reports submitted via the PRAIS
  reporting system; Trends.Earth sub-national outputs as a
  cross-check.
- **Cross-product:** AE-classified LC vs ESA CCI LC vs Copernicus
  CGLS-LC100; transition matrix application reproduces Trends.Earth
  result?
- **Confusion classes:** the LCCS / UNCCD aggregation collapses many
  ESA CCI classes; the country-defined transition matrix is what
  drives degradation determination, not the raw classification.
  Validation should test sensitivity to the transition matrix and
  the source classifier separately.
- **Stratification:** by ecoregion / FAO eco-zone.

## Caveats

1. **Country-defined transition matrix is the policy lever.** Two
   countries with identical AE classifications can report different
   15.3.1 values because their transition matrices score the same
   transition (e.g., forest → cropland) differently. AE
   classification only feeds the matrix.
2. **One-out-all-out is highly sensitive to false positives in any
   sub-indicator.** A pixel falsely flagged as degraded by sub-
   indicator 2 (productivity) propagates to the full indicator
   regardless of the LULC verdict. The LULC sub-component in
   isolation is not the full picture.
3. **AE pre-2017 baseline gap.** UNCCD baseline is typically 2000 or
   the start of the country's chosen reference period. AE only
   covers 2017+. For pre-2017 transitions, use ESA CCI LC or Hansen.
4. **Class aggregation choice.** Mapping ESA CCI's 22 classes to the
   UNCCD 6-class scheme involves judgement (mosaic classes,
   shrub/herbaceous boundary). Document the mapping table used.
5. **Spatial-support mismatch.** ESA CCI LC labels at 300 m used to
   supervise 10 m AE classification produce mixed-pixel label noise
   in heterogeneous landscapes; mitigate by sampling only labels
   within homogeneous-class neighbourhoods.
