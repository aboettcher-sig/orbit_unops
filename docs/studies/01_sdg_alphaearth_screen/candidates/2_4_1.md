# SDG 2.4.1 — Proportion of Agricultural Area Under Productive and Sustainable Agriculture

**Custodian:** FAO.
**Architecture letter:** **F** in full; **A** for the cropland-mask
denominator only.
**Verdict:** **PARTIAL** — only the agricultural-area denominator is
in scope under Study 01's narrow C1–C5 screen. The "productive and
sustainable" qualifier is a composite of 11 sub-themes (economic,
environmental, social) and is Architecture F under Study 02.

## Indicator (post-2025-review text)

> Proportion of agricultural area under productive and sustainable
> agriculture.

## What is in scope here

**Denominator:** total agricultural area within the AOI, classified
annually. AE classification of cropland + pasture against catalog
labels (ESA WorldCereal, ESA WorldCover crop class, USDA CDL, Canada
AAFC ACI, MapBiomas Agriculture). This is a defensible Architecture
A task on its own.

## What is out of scope here

**Numerator:** the share of agricultural area that meets FAO's 11
productive-and-sustainable sub-themes, including:

- Land productivity, profitability, resilience.
- Soil health, water use efficiency, fertiliser use.
- Pest management, biodiversity, GHG emissions.
- Wage rate, food security, land tenure.

These require farm-level survey data and admin/economic statistics.
No AE-based classification produces them.

## Recommendation

Treat 2.4.1 as a *feeder* to a Study 02 Architecture-F composer.
Build the AE-cropland classifier under Study 01; reserve the
sustainability-criterion composition for Study 02.
