# SDG 15.2.1 — Progress Towards Sustainable Forest Management

**Custodian:** FAO.
**Architecture letter:** **F** (composite); the forest-area-trend
sub-indicator is **A** (= 15.1.1); the biomass sub-indicator is **C**.
**Verdict:** **PARTIAL** — the forest-area-trend sub-indicator overlaps
15.1.1 and is in scope; the remaining sub-indicators are out of scope
under Study 01's narrow architecture.

## Indicator (post-2025-review text)

> Progress towards sustainable forest management.

## Measurement target (FAO methodology)

The indicator aggregates **five sub-indicators**:

1. **Forest area annual net change rate** — overlaps SDG 15.1.1.
2. **Above-ground biomass stock in forest** — continuous EO
   (GEDI L4A, ESA Biomass CCI). Architecture C.
3. **Proportion of forest area within legally established protected
   areas** — vector overlay (forest mask × WDPA). Architecture D.
4. **Proportion of forest area under a long-term forest management
   plan** — admin reporting. Out of scope.
5. **Forest area under an independent forest management certification
   scheme** — admin reporting (FSC, PEFC). Out of scope.

The country submission is a composite scorecard; sub-indicators 4 and
5 are admin-reported and never EO-derivable.

## Why this is PARTIAL

- Only sub-indicator 1 fits Study 01's architecture (and it is the
  same as 15.1.1).
- Sub-indicators 2, 3 are GEE-tractable but not via AE classification.
- Sub-indicators 4, 5 are admin.

The full indicator is Architecture F under Study 02.

the F-composer under Study 02 reusing the 15.1.1 forest mask.
